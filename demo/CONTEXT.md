## Limitations (say these out loud)
- It only knows what's written down. It can't see group chat, email, or Figma, and that is where half of a real team's state lives.
- Extraction from messy notes is imperfect. That's why claims are editable and confidence is shown.
- Matching "WCAG review" to "accessibility check" is a judgment call, so it's labelled Medium.
- It can open a conversation about workload. It can't tell you how anyone feels, so it never pretends to.

## Judge Q&A prep
- *"What if the tracker is right and the notes are wrong?"* The authority order is explicit, and the losing side is still shown.
- *"Privacy?"* Team data stays in the folder. Reasons for absence never leave stage 02. Own-project runs can use a local model.
- *"Why not a full PM platform?"* One team, one moment, one insight. That's the brief.
