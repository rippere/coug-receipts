# Voice — dignity rules (the 15% "Responsible AI" score lives here)

Test every line against this question: **would that teammate be comfortable seeing it about themselves, in front of the team?**

## Must
1. **Task first, person second.** Write "Demo request → Tyler, by Thu Oct 22". Never write "Tyler hasn't emailed Dana".
2. **Show constraints, never reasons.** Write "Kwame is away Oct 28–Nov 1". Leave out the wedding, the commute, the job, the illness, and the family matter. Stage 02 drops reasons. Stage 04 never adds them back.
3. **Every sentence has a receipt.** If it can't be traced to a file and a quote, it doesn't ship.
4. **Uncertainty is visible.** Every card shows a confidence level (High / Medium / Low) and one line saying why.
5. **Name the next step, not the fault.** "Pick one owner for the accessibility review" beats "two people think they own it".
6. **Ten-second budget.** `action` is at most 10 words and starts with a verb. `reason` is at most 22 words. One number per reason.

## Never
- Rank people, score people, or count anyone's absences or late items.
- Guess at motive, effort, or attitude ("seems disengaged", "dropped the ball").
- Suggest going to the instructor about a person. The brief and the charter both say *talk as a team first*. The tool can point to the charter's conflict plan.
- Draft or hint at **peer evaluation** content. Those are confidential to the instructor.
- Send private material (sponsor data, someone else's real project) to a cloud model without permission. Bonus own-project runs need the teammates' OK and can use a local model.

## Fair share panel
Show what each person is carrying this week, and list the **invisible work** (coordination, sponsor email, editing, design iterations) next to it. Brief §5 asks for exactly this in the contribution log. When load concentrates (one person holds ≥2× the median of open items, or blocks ≥2 other items), produce a **question for the team**, never a verdict:
> "Three M3 pieces wait on the coordinator screens. Can anyone take something off Mei's plate this week?"

## Talk-first tag
Set `talk_first: true` when a card or question is mainly about a person's load, availability, or a role gap. The board labels these cards "Bring to Tuesday's meeting" (or the group chat if the deadline comes first).
